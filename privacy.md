# Privacy Policy for Supreme System Discord Bot

*Last Updated: May 3, 2025*

## 1. Introduction

Thank you for using **Supreme System** (\"Bot\"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you use the Bot, we may collect the following data:
- **User IDs** (Discord snowflakes)
- **Server IDs** where the Bot is added
- **Command usage** (to improve features and monitor abuse)
- **Settings/configurations** set by server admins

We **do not** collect personal messages, passwords, or any sensitive personal information.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to:
- Provide and improve Bot features
- Monitor for abuse or misuse
- Perform moderation functions (if enabled)
- Maintain bot performance and uptime

## 4. Sharing of Data

- We **do not sell** or share your data with third parties.
- Data is only accessed by the Bot developers when needed for maintenance or abuse investigation.

## 5. Data Retention

- Basic usage logs and server settings are retained as long as necessary to operate the Bot.
- If you remove the Bot from your server, you can request deletion of stored data by contacting us.

## 6. Your Rights

- You can request access to the data we have about your server.
- You can request data deletion by emailing us at **liadprotopyt@gmail.com**.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in this document. Continued use of the Bot after changes indicates acceptance.

## 8. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at **liadprotopyt@gmail.com**.
